How does OpusClip work?

Upload a long video or paste a link. OpusClip finds the strongest moments, rearranges them into a short with captions, reframing and transitions, and scores each one for viral potential.

What types of videos can I upload?

Any kind. ClipAnything works on podcasts, interviews, vlogs, gaming, sports, TV and videos with little or no dialogue. You can also prompt it to find a specific moment.

Which languages are supported?

English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Japanese, Korean and more than 15 others, with more on the way.

Can I add captions?

Yes. Captions are added automatically with over 97% accuracy, and you can edit every word.

Is OpusClip free to use?

Yes. New users get a 7-day Pro trial with 90 minutes of processing (about 30 clips). After that, the free plan gives you 60 minutes every month, or you can upgrade.
